# Aangan Privacy Policy

**Effective 21 November 2026**

Aangan is a daily practice app for Hindu families, made by Himasagar Mallepalli
("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Aangan app for iPhone.
In short: **we have no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no server of our own. We cannot see your
practice, your family or your location.**

## What stays with you

**Your practice.** The days you light your diya, your japa counts, the verses and stories you read,
notes, pauses and your settings are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, Apple also keeps a copy in
**your own private iCloud database** so it syncs across your devices. Apple runs iCloud; we cannot
read that database. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

**Your family courtyard.** When you invite family, Aangan creates an iCloud share that only the
people you invite can open. Through it, members of your household see each other's chosen name and
colour, whether each person has lit their diya today, nudges, and progress in family story quests.
They never see anyone's streak count, missed days or pauses. The shared data lives in the iCloud
account of the person who created the household and is visible only to its members. We cannot read
it. Anyone holding the invite link can join, so share it only with family. If the organiser deletes
Aangan's iCloud data (see below), the household and everything shared in it are deleted for everyone.

**Kids' profiles** are just a name and age range saved on a parent's iPhone. Children don't have
accounts and we collect nothing from them.

**Your location.** If you allow it, Aangan uses your location once to find the nearest of the cities
it has festival dates for. It keeps only the city you choose, never your coordinates, and never sends
your location to us. iOS may contact Apple to turn coordinates into a place name. You can choose a city
by hand instead.

**Photos.** If you save an art card, Aangan asks for add-only access to your photo library. It cannot
see your photos.

**Notifications** are scheduled on your iPhone. We don't run a notification server.

## What third parties receive

**Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat.** Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. To check
whether you have Aangan Family, the app uses **RevenueCat**, a subscription service that acts as our
data processor. When the app starts or you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives:

- a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name, email or Apple Account),
- your App Store receipt and transaction details (what you bought, when, the price and currency, and
  when it renews or expires),
- technical details such as your device type, iOS version, app version, country and language, and the
  last time the app was used.

RevenueCat uses this to confirm your purchase and to give us totals such as the number of active
subscriptions. It stores the data on Amazon Web Services in the United States. We don't connect this
data to your identity, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Apple's handling of payments is
covered by Apple's privacy policy; we never see your payment details.

**YouTube.** Some festival guides show a few video thumbnails, loaded from YouTube's image server, and
links that open in the YouTube app or Safari. Loading a thumbnail or opening a video sends a normal web
request to Google, which receives your IP address and device details. Google's privacy policy applies
on YouTube. Nothing is embedded in Aangan, and we receive nothing from YouTube.

**Nobody else.** Aangan contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs.
If you choose to share crash reports with developers in iOS settings, Apple sends us anonymous crash
logs; you can turn that off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and export your data:** Settings › Privacy › "Export my practice history" saves everything
  Aangan stores about your practice as a JSON file.
- **Delete your data:** delete the app to remove the data on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, go
  to the iPhone's Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) ›
  Aangan › Delete Data. For the organiser of a family courtyard, this also deletes the household.
- **Purchase data held by RevenueCat:** because the ID is anonymous, we can find it only with the
  details you give us. Email us from the address below with a screenshot of your Aangan purchase
  receipt (from Apple's email) and we will ask RevenueCat to delete the matching record.
- **Location, photos, notifications:** change them any time in Settings › Aangan.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, California or India), you have rights to
access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your
data protection authority. Because we hold almost nothing about you, most requests are answered by the
steps above; for anything else, email us. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on performing
our contract with you (providing the app and your purchases) and our legitimate interest in knowing
how many people subscribe.

## Children

Aangan is suitable for families and children can use it on a parent's iPhone. We don't knowingly
collect personal information from children, and the app has no accounts, chat or advertising. Kid mode
shows only YouTube links we have checked, and they open outside the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release
notes. If a change would let us collect something new, we will ask you in the app first.
