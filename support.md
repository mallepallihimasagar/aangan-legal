# Aangan support

Write to us at **[himasagar.appledev@gmail.com](mailto:himasagar.appledev@gmail.com)**. We usually answer within two days. Please tell us your
iPhone model, iOS version and the Aangan version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?**
Open Family › Settings (the gear) › Membership › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you
bought with. If you are in an Apple Family Sharing group with the buyer, Aangan Family unlocks
automatically after a restore.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Aangan › Cancel Subscription. You keep Aangan Family
until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

**My family member can't join the courtyard.**
Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on (iPhone Settings › [your name] ›
iCloud). Send the invite again from Family › Invite family, or let them scan the QR code. A courtyard
holds up to 6 people.

**Why don't my family members see Aangan Family features?**
Aangan Family is shared through Apple Family Sharing. Courtyard members who aren't in your Apple family
use the free features. This is an App Store rule, not our choice.

**A festival date is different from my temple's.**
Dates are calculated for the city you chose (Settings › Daily reminder shows it) and for Purnimanta or
Amanta months (Settings › Practice). Check both first. Communities sometimes observe a different day;
follow your temple. If you think we've made a mistake, email us the festival, your city and your
temple's date.

**I missed a day. Is my streak gone?**
No. You get grace days each month, and you can pause your diya for up to 13 days for travel, illness,
sutak or any reason. Paused days never break your count.

**How do I move or delete my data?**
Settings › Privacy › Export my practice history saves a file of everything. To delete, remove the app
and then delete Aangan's data in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
