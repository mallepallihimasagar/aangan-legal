# Aangan

A daily diya for Hindu families who live far from home. Light a diya each day after a moment of
practice, follow festival and fasting dates for your city, read the Bhagavad Gita verse by verse and
keep your family in step, wherever each of you lives.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Coming to the App Store for iPhone.
